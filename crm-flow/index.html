What each section does

Login & account Email + password login. New users can self-serve sign up, but must confirm via the link in their email before login succeeds.
Dashboard The first-glance overview: open deals, total pipeline value, deals won this month, and open task count, plus a chart broken down by stage. All read-only, computed live — nothing here is manually maintained.
Contacts The record of people you work with. The list is searchable; a contact's detail view shows every deal and task linked to them, plus an Activity timeline you can comment on.
Pipeline The sales process as a kanban board across six stages. Drag a card to change its stage — the system logs that automatically. Open a card to see the deal's full history.
Tasks Follow-ups, optionally linked to a contact or a deal. Overdue and due-soon items are visually called out.
Activity timeline The shared mechanism behind Contacts and Pipeline: system events (creation, stage changes) and manual comments sit in one timeline, newest first — effectively a record's history.

A typical walkthrough

  1. Log in as demo@crm-demo.test and land in the app shell.
  2. Start at Dashboard to see the pipeline's overall shape — how much is open, its total value, how many deals closed this month.
  3. Open Pipeline and drag a deal from Proposal to Negotiation — a stage-change entry appears on its timeline automatically.
  4. Open that deal's detail view and post a comment on its Activity timeline — e.g. "Customer asked for an extension."
  5. Go to Contacts and find the linked contact — their detail view shows every deal and task tied to them.
  6. Go to Tasks and add a follow-up, linked to that deal, with a due date.
  7. Back on Dashboard, the numbers and chart already reflect everything you just did.
Colors correspond to each section (see above). The Activity timeline's dashed arrows mark "writes to" — dragging a card to a new stage and posting a comment both add an entry to the same timeline.
